The 118th Holy Convocation is underway — "Answering The Call"

Thousands of saints are gathered in St. Louis this week for the 118th Holy Convocation, the church's largest annual meeting. Services run November 3–8 at America's Center, and about 60,000 delegates are expected across the week.

It's COGIC's first St. Louis convocation since 2019 — and the city is now the gathering's home through 2028, after delegates voted in January 2025 to bring it back. The week closes with the General Assembly, November 9–10.

Leadership & What's Ahead

The Church Today

Presiding Bishop

Bishop J. Drew Sheard Sr. leads the church today — elected March 27, 2021, and re-elected by acclamation on November 12, 2024 to a second four-year term as Presiding Bishop and Chief Apostle.

Home Base

Mason Temple in Memphis remains the church's international headquarters and spiritual home — even as the Holy Convocation gathers in St. Louis through 2028.

The Church Today

A reported 6.5 million members in more than 100 countries — thousands of congregations, one faith, gathered every year at the Holy Convocation, the church's largest annual meeting.

Nov 9–10

General Assembly — St. Louis

The 118th Holy Convocation closes with the General Assembly, November 9–10, 2026, in St. Louis — the church's governing body in session as convocation week wraps up.

2027

Convocation returns to St. Louis

Year two of the St. Louis run. Delegates voted in January 2025 to hold the Holy Convocation in St. Louis for 2026–2028, its home from 2010 to 2019.

2028

Final year of the St. Louis run

The third St. Louis convocation in a row — and the last under the current vote. Where the gathering goes next will be a story of its own.
